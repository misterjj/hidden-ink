# Hidden Ink

**Secret GM notes and stat blocks on your tokens and maps.** Everything the GM writes is encrypted in their browser before it reaches the scene: players cannot read it, not even with their browser's developer tools.

![Hidden Ink](https://hidden-ink.pages.dev/store/hero.jpg)

## Features

- **Stat blocks on tokens:** search the SRD with Open5e, or write one by hand. Move, resize, or minimize the window to a row of icons.
- **Secret notes on any token**, including a player's own token: they never see it.
- **Map notes** for traps, hidden items and mechanisms, with Markdown, a "done" state, titles only the GM sees, and a searchable list.
- **Real secrecy:** stat blocks and notes are encrypted with AES-GCM 256. The key stays in the GM's browser.
- **English and French.**

## How to use

1. Right-click a token: **Add a stat block** or **Add a secret note**.
2. For map notes, pick the **Map notes** tool, click the map, and choose a type.
3. Open the settings (gear icon in the Map notes tool) and **export your encryption key**. Keep it safe: you need it on another computer or to share with a co-GM.
